# G-FLO 2026 price list — what was imported, and what needs your rates

**196 products** were transcribed from *G-FLO Price List 2026* (20-page Canva PDF, 11 Aug 2026) and loaded into the catalogue. 118 have a printed rate. **78 do not** — the printed list shows those cells blank or as `00`, so they were imported as *“Price on request”* rather than ₹0.

Fill them in whenever you're ready: Admin → Products → filter **Price = On request**, then type the rate straight into the table. Until then the storefront shows “Price on request” and leaves them out of cart totals.

## Two duplicate codes in the printed list

Page 17 reuses codes that pages 8 and 12 already use:

| Printed code | Used on | Also used on | How it was imported |
|---|---|---|---|
| GF-3101 – GF-3107 | p8 — Drilling Machine, Angle Grinder, Screw Machine | p17 — Flexible Pipe 10–50 mm | Flexible pipe SKUs got a `-FP-WG` / `-FP-BK` suffix (white-grey / black) |
| GF-3201 – GF-3204 | p8 — Waterproof immersion rods | p17 — PVC Wall Plug 25–50 mm | Wall plug SKUs got a `-WP` suffix |

SKUs have to be unique, so the suffixes keep both sets addressable. If you'd rather renumber one
range properly, edit the SKU on each product in the admin panel — nothing else depends on it.

## Judgement calls worth checking

- **Fan rods** were split into one product per length (17 sizes × 2 grades = 34 products), each with
  its own printed rate, rather than one product with a size dropdown. Easier to price and stock.
- **Multi-rate rows** became one product per variant — e.g. Tester (Small ₹15 / Big ₹25) is two
  products, Plier (Sparton ₹130 / Marc ₹110 / Normal ₹80) is three.
- **“Waterproof Triangle / Ghadi / Normal”** (GF-3201–3203) were read as immersion water heaters from
  the photos and named *Immersion Rod — …*. Rename if that's wrong.
- **Screws** are priced per 1000 pcs, cable ties and nail clips per packet, flexible pipe per coil —
  the unit is stored on each product and shown next to the price.
- **Machine Screw (GF-4101)** is ₹145/kg with six lengths listed; imported as one product priced per
  kg with the sizes in the description.
- **Menscore (GF-4211)** is a 14-row table with no rates; imported as 14 products, all on request.
- Product photos were lifted from the PDF itself (161 of 196 products have one). A handful of
  photo-to-product matches on dense pages are worth a glance — swap any that look wrong via
  Admin → Products → Edit → Photos.
